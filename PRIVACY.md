# Privacy Policy - Uzma YT Automation

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Uzma YT Automation is a personal tool used only by its owner, Uzma Azreen, to upload videos and manage comments on her own YouTube channel "Uzma Azreen Studio".

**What the app accesses:** only the owner's own YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API, to upload videos and read or reply to comments on those videos.

**What we collect:** nothing from other users or viewers. The app does not collect, sell, share or store any personal data of anyone else. The owner's access token is kept privately as an encrypted GitHub secret and is never shared.

**YouTube API Services:** this app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
